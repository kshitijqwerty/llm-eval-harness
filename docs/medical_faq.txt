MISSED DOSES
If you miss a dose, take it as soon as you remember.
If it is almost time for your next dose, skip the missed dose.
Never take two doses at once to make up for a missed one.

ALCOHOL AND ANTIBIOTICS
Alcohol should be avoided while taking antibiotics.
Alcohol can reduce the effectiveness of the medication and worsen side effects.
Some antibiotics like metronidazole cause severe reactions when combined with alcohol.

IBUPROFEN SIDE EFFECTS
Common side effects include stomach upset, nausea, heartburn, and dizziness.
Long-term use can cause serious side effects including gastrointestinal bleeding and kidney problems.
Take ibuprofen with food or milk to reduce stomach upset.

INSULIN STORAGE
Unopened insulin vials and pens should be stored in the refrigerator between 2-8 degrees Celsius.
Once opened, insulin can be stored at room temperature below 25 degrees Celsius for up to 28 days.
Never freeze insulin or expose it to direct sunlight or heat.

CHEST PAIN EMERGENCY
Seek emergency care immediately for chest pain accompanied by shortness of breath.
Other warning signs include sweating, nausea, lightheadedness, or pain radiating to the arm, neck, or jaw.
These symptoms may indicate a heart attack and require immediate medical attention.
Do not drive yourself — call emergency services.
